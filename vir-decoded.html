WCVIS Texas Education Series

VIR Decoded.

An education series I launched with WCVIS Texas to make vascular and interventional radiology more accessible to trainees, from pre-meds to residents. Each session walks through how VIR actually works as a clinical specialty, with real cases, interactive questions, and a look inside the VIR suite.

Created and presented by Yaroslav Vorobyov · WCVIS Texas Education Chair

SESSION 01

The IR Consult, Preprocedural Planning & Life in the VIR Suite

VIR is not a room; it is a clinical workflow. This session follows a patient from referral to follow-up, built around a peripheral arterial disease case.

  1. ConsultEvaluate a new consult
  2. PreprocedureScreen for risk
  3. VIR SuiteExplain the imaging environment
  4. ProcedureApproach a fluoroscopic image
  5. PostprocedureRecognize warning signs
